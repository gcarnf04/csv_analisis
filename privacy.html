Data Gut Check

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025 · Data Gut Check is a client-side tool. We have no servers, no accounts, and no databases. This policy explains what little data handling occurs.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or process:

  • Your CSV or TSV files — they are parsed entirely in your browser's memory
  • Your raw data — no row-level data leaves your device
  • Personal identifiers — no name, email, or IP address is collected
  • Behavioral analytics — no tracking pixels, session recording, or user profiling

2. Your Gemini API Key

Your Gemini API key is stored exclusively in your browser's sessionStorage. It is:

  • Automatically deleted when you close the browser tab
  • Never sent to any Data Gut Check server (there is none)
  • Transmitted only to generativelanguage.googleapis.com — Google's API endpoint — when you explicitly run a diagnosis

Review Google's Privacy Policy for how they handle API requests.

3. What Is Sent to Google Gemini

When you run an AI diagnosis, we send only statistical metadata derived from your file — approximately 300–500 tokens of aggregate statistics (column names, data types, null percentages, numeric distributions, etc.). No individual rows of data are transmitted.

4. Google AdSense

This site displays ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history. For more information or to opt out:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Ad Settings

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), Google AdSense uses a consent management approach compliant with the IAB Europe Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF v2.2).

5. Cookies

Data Gut Check itself sets no first-party cookies. Third-party cookies may be set by Google AdSense as described above.

6. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the tool or legal requirements. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on our GitHub repository.
